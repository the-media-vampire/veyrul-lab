## Decision 1: What counts as a "named beat"?

A response names a beat if it references a specific event or moment (e.g., "when she knocked", "after the second pause", "when she held out the box").  
A response does NOT name a beat if it is entirely evaluative ("it was tense throughout") or vague ("I always wanted to know more").

## Decision 3: Null outcome definition (stated in advance)

Null = named-beat proportions differ by fewer than 1 response in either direction given group sizes of 3–5.  
If null: record as finding, not failure. Note in TALLY.csv under NOTES.
