The door had been shut for three days. No one on the street would say why.
On the fourth morning a child walked up to it, raised her hand, and knocked.
[PAUSE]
She knocked a second time.
[PAUSE]
The door opened.
A very old woman stood inside, holding something the child had never seen before.
She held it out.
[PAUSE]
The child did not reach for it.

[End of Version B]

Pause positions: after first knock, after second knock, after the held-out object — each a moment of unresolved expectation.
