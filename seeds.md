# Story Seeds

## Seed H — Humdrum premise
A postal worker sorts the morning's letters, finds one addressed to herself, sets it aside, finishes the route, returns to the depot, sits in the break room, and opens the letter.

This premise is chosen because each step is ordinary and immediately plausible without explanation.

## Seed Z — Bizarre premise
A woman wakes to find the furniture in her apartment has rearranged itself overnight into a configuration she does not recognize but that feels, somehow, like a room she has never been in before.

This premise is chosen because the core event resists easy belief; it needs either anchoring or vivid rendering to be accepted.

## Why these seeds
- Both are single-scene, low-character, short enough to write in ~150–250 words
- Seed H is entirely explainable by routine; Seed Z cannot be explained within the scene
- Matching length target keeps word count from being a confound
