# Inspection protocol

Follow this order. Do not skip steps.

## Step 1: Concealed-label playback

Assign random labels (A/B/C/D) to the four arms before watching.
Watch full-frame, muted, at normal speed, same display size.
Note any playback anomaly by label only. Do not reveal arm mapping yet.

## Step 2: Crop inspection (after full-frame)

Extract matched still frames at three moments:
- T1: 1 second before the head turn begins
- T2: mid-turn (peak motion)
- T3: 1 second after turn settles

For each moment, inspect three crops:

| Crop | What to record (observation only) |
|---|---|
| Wall (background, no face) | Is pixel-level flicker visible between frames? |
| Eyes + cheek | Is edge definition stable? |
| Turning silhouette | Is a trailing or duplicated contour visible? |

Record as: "[arm label] T2 silhouette: trailing edge visible / not visible"
Do not write taste judgments here.

## Step 3: Reveal labels and map to arms

Only after all observation rows are filled.

## Step 4: Taste judgments (separate section)

Write these after label reveal, clearly marked as judgment:
- "The face feels less present in arm long" is a judgment.
- "A trailing edge is visible at T2 silhouette in arm long" is an observation.

## Decision rule (from Iris, preserved)

1. Reject any arm where motion artifacts are visible at normal playback.
2. Among remaining arms, choose least filtering that reduces background distraction
   without weakening the gesture.
3. Keep control if no treatment earns its place.
