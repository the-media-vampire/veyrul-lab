VERSION A — Expository (control)

Mira was the kind of person who noticed things others ignored.
She was generous by instinct, though sometimes impatient.
The old man across the aisle was clearly confused about which stop
was his, and Mira, being helpful, leaned over and told him.

---
Note: Character traits are stated directly — "generous by instinct,"
"impatient," "helpful." The narrator does the interpretive work
for the reader. This matches what Parr warns against: explaining,
digressing, telling the reader a character is clever or stupid.
