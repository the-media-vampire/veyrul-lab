VERSION B — Action and dialogue (test condition)

The old man lifted his head each time the train slowed,
then settled back without getting up.
Mira watched him do it three times.
"Ridgewood?" she asked.
He looked at her.
"Two stops," she said. "I'll tell you."
He nodded and looked back at the window.
She watched the stops for him the rest of the way.

---
Note: No trait is named. The same information — attentive,
willing to act, matter-of-fact rather than warm — is carried
by what Mira does and says. The reader supplies the label,
or does not.
