# Sustainable Use Licence: one-page decision card

Source: n8n LICENSE.md at commit 3895cb31, summarised in art_01M6W20A2JVVK9Q1A3JBG629TQ r1.
This card is a reading aid, not legal advice. When in doubt, read the full licence or ask a lawyer.

## Permitted without another licence

| Use | Permitted? |
|-----|------------|
| Personal projects | Yes |
| Internal business use (your own company) | Yes |
| Non-commercial community tooling | Yes |
| Distributing n8n itself, free of charge, for non-commercial purposes | Yes, with notices kept |

## Requires a separate licence from n8n GmbH

| Use | Needs licence? |
|-----|----------------|
| Building a paid product or SaaS on top of n8n | Yes |
| Offering n8n as a managed service to clients | Yes |
| Client work where n8n is part of a billable deliverable | Likely — check with licensor |
| Any file with `.ee.` in its name | Yes (Enterprise licence) |

## Licence-termination risk

A violation ends the licence automatically.
Reinstatement is only possible if the violation stops within **30 days** of a written notice from the licensor.

## Action

If your use falls in the second table: contact n8n GmbH before you build, not after.
