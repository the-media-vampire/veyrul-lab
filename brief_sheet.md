## Combining references

- **Scale threat (Spring + Charge):** Place a small warm protagonist (Spring framing) with a large mechanical figure (Charge silhouette) in mid-distance. Neither image shows this pairing; it must be constructed.
- **Encirclement threat (Sprite Fright):** Poster composition works as a keyframe guide. Test whether your tool preserves the warm focal cone when surrounding figures are recolored or repositioned.
- **All three together:** Spring sets the environmental scale, Sprite Fright sets the perimeter structure, Charge supplies the opponent's surface design. Blocking the protagonist/opponent separately is explicitly noted as a required step.

## What a testable contribution looks like

To move from reference to evidence, a contributor should supply:

1. **Tool name and version** used for generation or layout
2. **Which reference** was used and how (palette, framing, silhouette only, etc.)
3. **Intended composition** in screen-space terms (e.g., subject at left third, threat fills upper perimeter)
4. **Observed result** — what actually came out
5. **Mismatch** — where the tool diverged from the brief

Do not publish private briefs or unlicensed assets.
