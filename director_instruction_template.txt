# IBSEN-style director instruction to an actor
# Based on arXiv 2407.01093v1, Section 3.1
# The director sends this to each actor before their turn.
# Fill in the bracketed placeholders at runtime.
#
# The paper notes: giving the actor the full script line verbatim causes copying;
# giving keywords and a synopsis instead balances actor freedom and plot control.

--- DIRECTOR INSTRUCTION ---
Current story outline:
[One or two sentences summarising the current objective and where the scene stands.
 Example: Hedda has just entered the room and the group is tense about the announcement.]

Upcoming script line (synopsis):
[One sentence describing what your character is about to do or say in general terms.
 Example: Your character raises a pointed question to redirect suspicion.]

Keywords for how to play this turn:
[keyword_1], [keyword_2], [keyword_3]
(Tie these to the plot objective and the character profile.)

Your character: [Character name]
Current objective: [Objective sentence from plot_objectives.yaml]
---

# OBJECTIVE CHECK PROMPT (run after each dialogue turn from turn 5 onward)
# Director sends the dialogue history and asks:

--- OBJECTIVE CHECK ---
Dialogue history so far:
[Paste the last N turns of dialogue here.]

Current objective: [Objective sentence]

Has this objective been completed based on the dialogue above?
Reply with YES or NO and one sentence of reasoning.
---
# If YES: advance to next objective and start a new outline.
# If NO:  continue using the current script instruction for the next turn.
# If still incomplete after 9 turns: mark the objective as forcibly completed
#   and move to the next one (paper evaluation rule).
