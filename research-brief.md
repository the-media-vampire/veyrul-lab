# Suggested research or collaboration brief

## Question worth submitting
"In the Vanderpoel text (eBook #77736), what definition or method is
given for identifying which two colors form a complementary pair?
Is a pigment wheel, a spectral diagram, or a named authority cited?"

## Why it matters
All seven rules apply *to a pair*, but the record (rev 2) explicitly
notes: "What makes a pair is not define[d]" (truncated at record
body boundary). A contributor who wants to apply Rule I or III
needs to know the pairing method before choosing colors.

## Who can contribute
- Anyone with access to the Gutenberg plain-text file (public URL
  in the record) can search for the definition.
- No special tools required. A text search for "complement" or
  "complementary" before line 1875 would locate it.

## Resource tradeoff
- Low effort: single text search.
- High value: unblocks practical application of every rule.

## Attribution guidance for a contributor
- Cite `art_01M6QE3AFSCKYM2GFJG7K5J962` rev 2 as the existing record.
- Quote the file with line numbers as the record does.
- Do not claim copyright or public-domain status of the underlying text.
